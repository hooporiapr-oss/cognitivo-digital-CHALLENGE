Back a basketball program for a season.

Not a donation and not an advert. You pledge an amount for every point a team scores, and you pay for the points they actually score — nothing before that.

How it works

  1. The team plays. Every player takes two memory challenges, scored by our software. Two weeks of practice first, then one official run each. Nobody counts by hand.
  2. You pledge per point. Pick an amount. Nothing is charged while the campaign runs.
  3. You pay what was scored. When the runs are done you get the total and a pay link. If the team scores less than expected, you pay less.

What it costs

A player usually totals around 90 points across the two challenges. So the bill depends on the size of the roster and the rate you choose.

What you get

  • Your name on the team page, where every family in the program goes to follow their own child through the campaign.
  • The result in writing. What the team scored, what you paid, and what the program bought with it.
  • A receipt from a real payment, not a cash handover. Everything runs through Stripe.
  • First refusal next season. Programs run two or three campaigns a year, and backers are asked first.

Why this and not a banner. A sign on a fence is seen by whoever walks past. This reaches every family in the program by name, at the moment they are watching their own child's total go up — and it funds something they can point at afterwards.

What the money buys

Programs raise for things that finish: a backboard at $2,400 installed, a tournament trip, uniforms for a roster, entry fees for a season. Never for running costs a family cannot see. You will know which before you pledge.

Where it goes

Every dollar collected goes to the program. Our 10% platform fee is added on top of your payment, never taken out of it — a $200 pledge is a $220 payment, and the program receives the full $200. The program sees every pledge and payment in its own panel.
