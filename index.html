What it costs, and what you see

  1. Nothing until the game is played. A pledge is a promise. Nothing is charged before game day, and a player who never plays is never charged for.
  2. The padrino pays, the program is paid. When the game is over they get the box score and a pay button. Minimum $5 a pledge.
  3. You watch the money as it arrives. Every pledge, payment and payout, listed player by player in your own panel. Our 20% platform fee is added on top of their payment, never taken out of it. A $25.00 pledge is a $30.00 payment, and the program receives the full $25.00 collected.

You see all of it as it happens — every pledge, every payment, every payout, listed for you and for the program.
