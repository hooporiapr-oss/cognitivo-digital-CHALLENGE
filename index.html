What is a padrino?

Someone who backs a student personally. A parent, an aunt, a neighbour, a former coach, a family friend. The word means godparent, and that is the idea.

Not a company buying its name on a banner — a person who decided this student is worth backing. Most students find five or six.

Chain scoring.

Each round adds one more, and each round is worth more than the last. Complete round four and it banks four points; round nine banks nine. The score is the running total, which means it is an honest count of what the student actually did.

That is why one more round matters so much. Going from ten to twelve is not a fifth better — it is 55 points to 78, a forty percent jump in what that student raises. The effort at the edge of what they can hold is worth the most.
