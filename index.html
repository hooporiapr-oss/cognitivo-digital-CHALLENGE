How it runs

  1. Two weeks of pledging. Each participant shares one link. Padrinos pick their amount per point. Nothing is charged.
  2. One run day. Practice all they like, then one official run. The software scores it.
  3. Padrinos pay, the program is paid. Each padrino gets the score and a pay button. Our 10% platform fee is added on top of their payment, never taken out of it. A $25.00 pledge is a $27.50 payment, and the program receives the full $25.00 collected.

You see all of it as it happens — every pledge, every payment, every payout, listed for your program. Nothing counts as raised until it has actually been collected.
