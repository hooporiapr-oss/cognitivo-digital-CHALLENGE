One page your families will actually check.

Your schedule, your results and your running points total, on a link you send once. The next game sits at the top with a pledge button under it — so every game is its own small ask, and every result is a reason to look again.

What it costs, and what you see

  1. Nothing until a game is played. A pledge is a promise. No card is charged until you enter a score.
  2. The padrino pays, the program is paid. After each game they get the score and a pay button. Minimum $5 a pledge.
  3. You watch the money as it arrives. Every pledge, payment and payout, listed per game in your own panel. Our 20% platform fee is added on top of their payment, never taken out of it. A $25.00 pledge is a $30.00 payment, and the program receives the full $25.00 collected.

You see all of it as it happens — every pledge, every payment, every payout, listed for you and for the program.
