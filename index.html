FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long is a campaign?

Players recruit pledgers for 14 days. The game is played on day 15, followed by automatic notifications and final reporting.

How is each pledge calculated?

The final team score is multiplied by each supporter's per-point commitment, subject to their selected maximum pledge cap.

Does Cada Punto Cuenta handle proceeds?

No. Cada Punto Cuenta manages pledge commitments and reports only. The participating organization independently manages all proceeds.

How is the software licensed?

One fixed-fee software license covers one team and one game. There is no upfront license payment; the agreed fee is invoiced after event closing.
