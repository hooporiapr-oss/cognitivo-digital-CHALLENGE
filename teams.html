Sponsors pledge per point

A player shares their link. Family, neighbors and followers pledge any amount per point — a dime, a quarter, a dollar. Nothing is charged when they pledge.

Players practise free

All three games are open to anyone, any time, with nothing recorded. Nobody goes into their run cold.

One official run each

The score is the longest chain they hold. Reach level 12 and that is 78 points. Our software counts it, so there is nothing to argue about.

Invoices go out, the program gets paid

Each sponsor is billed their rate times the points scored, with thirty days to pay. The program receives half of everything collected.
